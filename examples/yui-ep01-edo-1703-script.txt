EPISODE 1

EXT. NIHONBASHI BRIDGE - MORNING
Characters: Yui

△ Yui walks into frame from the edge, holding her phone up at arm's length in a selfie pose, moving through the crowd of Edo-period townspeople and samurai crossing the wooden bridge.
△ She looks around at the wooden bridge and canal in disbelief.

YUI: Okay. Edo. Seventeen-oh-three. And I'm wearing… my school uniform.

△ A flat wooden cargo boat drifts slowly along the canal beneath the bridge.
△ A dark blue noren curtain hanging from a nearby shopfront flutters and billows in the wind.
△ A street vendor carrying a shoulder pole with baskets crosses the bridge in the background, unhurried.


EXT. MERCHANT STREET - MORNING
Characters: Yui

△ The street is empty. Yui walks into frame from the right side, holding her phone up in a selfie pose, moving through the narrow Edo-period merchant street lined with wooden shopfronts and paper lanterns.
△ A vendor at a shopfront pauses mid-task and stares at her modern school uniform.

YUI: Everyone's staring. Like, everyone.


EXT. CLOTH SHOP DOORWAY - MORNING
Characters: Yui

△ Yui ducks through a dark blue noren curtain into the entrance of a small cloth merchant's shop, the curtain falling closed behind her.

YUI: Fine. Fine. Wardrobe change.

△ A moment later, Yui steps back out through the noren curtain, now dressed in an indigo cotton kosode robe tied with a red obi sash, her hair gathered into a low bun. The red kumihimo cord bracelet is still visible on her left wrist.
△ She holds her phone up in a selfie pose, then tilts it down to show off her new outfit.

YUI: Honestly? Kind of a vibe.


EXT. FISH MARKET - MORNING
Characters: Yui, Vendor

△ Yui walks through the crowded fish market alley past wooden stalls, baskets of fish, and vendors calling out, holding her phone up in a selfie pose.

YUI: Fun fact: Edo's about to be the biggest city on Earth. Like, a million people.

△ Yui passes the fish stall of an elderly vendor woman. The Vendor looks up from her work and calls out.

VENDOR: Irasshai!

△ Yui, surprised and pleased, gives a small bow.

YUI: Arigatou.

△ Yui straightens up and turns back toward the camera with a delighted expression.


EXT. ROOFTOPS - MORNING
Characters:

△ Thin wisps of haze drift slowly across the dark tiled rooftops of the Nihonbashi district.
△ A small boat glides along the distant canal between the buildings.
△ A flock of birds crosses the sky toward Mount Fuji, faint on the horizon.


EXT. HILLSIDE BELL TOWER - AFTERNOON
Characters: Yui, Samurai

△ Yui walks up the stone stairs onto the temple terrace and stops at the wooden railing overlooking the town below, holding her phone up in a selfie pose.
△ A samurai walks past behind her, crossing the terrace.
△ Yui glances at him, then turns back to the camera with a thoughtful expression.

YUI: Nobody here knows what's coming. They're just living.

△ The red cord bracelet on her left wrist catches the light and glows faintly. A soft white lens flare briefly washes over the frame.
