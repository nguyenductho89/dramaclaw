EPISODE 1

EXT. PRESCHOOL PLAYGROUND - DAY
Characters: Little Trump, Little Kim

△ Little Trump places the final brick on a low toy wall and admires his work.

LITTLE TRUMP: The biggest wall in playground history. Nobody gets in without my say-so.

△ Little Kim raises his toy rocket.

LITTLE KIM (flat, polite): A wall. Charming. I brought a rocket, just in case.

LITTLE TRUMP: It's a toy rocket.

LITTLE KIM: And that's a toy wall.

△ Little Trump pauses, then quietly adds another brick to the wall.

△ Little Trump extends his hand.

LITTLE TRUMP: Let's make a deal. A huge deal.

LITTLE KIM: Lovely. You take down the wall, I'll put down the rocket.

△ Little Trump glances at his wall, then at the rocket.

LITTLE TRUMP: Let's not rush things.

△ Little Kim points at a box of candy beside Little Trump.

LITTLE KIM: And you can give me my sweets back.

△ Little Trump pulls the candy box against his chest.

LITTLE TRUMP: I'm not taking your candy. I'm keeping it safe. For national security.

LITTLE KIM: From what?

LITTLE TRUMP: You.

△ Little Kim slowly raises his rocket.

LITTLE KIM: Naturally. I'll keep mine ready, too.

△ Little Trump straightens up proudly.

LITTLE TRUMP: We'll sign the greatest peace deal in playground history. Got any paper?

△ Little Kim pulls a napkin from his pocket.

LITTLE KIM: Will this do?

△ Little Trump produces a crayon drawing of his wall.

LITTLE TRUMP: I've got a blueprint.

△ Little Kim examines the drawing.

LITTLE KIM: That's four bricks and a door.

△ Little Trump snatches the drawing back.

LITTLE TRUMP: Classified.

△ Little Kim looks at his napkin, then folds it into his pocket.

LITTLE KIM: Best not put that in writing, then.

△ The two boys stare at each other. Neither puts down his toy.

LITTLE KIM: Well, I suppose we'll have to trust each other.

LITTLE TRUMP: Absolutely.

△ Little Trump offers his hand.

LITTLE TRUMP: You first.

LITTLE KIM: After you.

△ They finally shake hands solemnly. Neither lets go of his toy.

LITTLE TRUMP AND LITTLE KIM: Peace!

△ They hold the handshake a little too long.

△ Little Kim glances at the wall, the candy box, and the rocket.

LITTLE KIM: So, what changes?

△ Little Trump smiles broadly without loosening his grip on the candy.

LITTLE TRUMP: Let's not ruin the announcement.

△ They continue shaking hands. The wall remains standing. The candy stays put. The rocket stays raised.
